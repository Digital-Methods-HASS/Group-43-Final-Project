# Sources used in the paper

These are the historical, methodological, and website sources used to support the revised final project. The exact lyric webpages used when the dataset was created were not recorded, and the project does not assign sources afterwards without evidence.

## Historical and scholarly sources

- Burstein, Paul, and William Freudenburg. 1978. “Changing Public Policy: The Impact of Public Opinion, Antiwar Demonstrations, and War Costs on Senate Voting on Vietnam War Motions.” *American Journal of Sociology* 84 (1): 99–122.
- Eyerman, Ron, and Andrew Jamison. 1998. *Music and Social Movements: Mobilizing Traditions in the Twentieth Century*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press. Publisher page: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/music-and-social-movements/16F59C7E95CA84679C49D320EF2B6460
- Kramer, Michael J. 2013. *The Republic of Rock: Music and Citizenship in the Sixties Counterculture*. Oxford: Oxford University Press.
- Lynskey, Dorian. 2011. *33 Revolutions per Minute: A History of Protest Songs*. New York: Ecco.
- Mueller, John E. 1971. “Trends in Popular Support for the Wars in Korea and Vietnam.” *American Political Science Review* 65 (2): 358–375. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2307/1954454
- Silge, Julia, and David Robinson. 2017. *Text Mining with R: A Tidy Approach*. Sebastopol, CA: O’Reilly Media. Open online version: https://www.tidytextmining.com/

## Historical and website material

- National Archives. “Episode 9: Crossing into Cambodia.” *Remembering Vietnam*. https://www.archives.gov/exhibits/remembering-vietnam-online-exhibit-episodes-9-12
- Billboard. Creedence Clearwater Revival chart history: https://www.billboard.com/artist/creedence-clearwater-revival/chart-history/hsi/
- Billboard. John Lennon chart history: https://www.billboard.com/artist/john-lennon/chart-history/hsi/

## Primary material and project files

- `data/lyrics.csv`: the twelve lyric records contained in the original DAM Exam submission.
- `data/metadata.csv`: title, artist, year, period, word count, and source note for each record.

## Archive note

Genius and AZLyrics were used as lyric resources, but the exact page used for each song was not preserved. The project states this limitation directly rather than presenting newly located pages as the original sources.
